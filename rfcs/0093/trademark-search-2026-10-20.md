Close marks, lower risk: TEKION and TEKION AI (EU and US, classes 9 and 42; the
owner has opposed a TEKIO mark before), Teklo, TEKKIO, Tukio, WITEKIO.
Bulgaria has no TEKIO or ТЕКИО mark. An AI company using the name TEKIO holds no
registered mark anywhere.

**Reading.** Class 42 is clear in the EU. Class 9 carries two live, identical
EU marks for unrelated goods, so a class 9 filing invites an opposition and
needs narrow terms and a lawyer's check. In the US, Tekio Labs' filing covers
almost exactly this product.
