# Name candidates, 2026-10-20

Why: the [TEKIO trademark search](trademark-search-2026-10-20.md) found a US
filing for the same kind of app. These are alternatives, sourced from two
places: the word *adapt* (or its nearest sense) in many languages, favouring
dead, minority and island languages and words tied to adapting through
practice; and Bulgarian words in the domain (Old Bulgarian, modern Bulgarian,
words Bulgarian took in).

**Stage 1, this file: language and web screen.** Meaning, origin, how the word
reads in English and Bulgarian, and whether a fitness or health product already
uses it, found by web search. **Stage 2, pending:** trademark registers (TMview
across EUIPO, Bulgaria, WIPO and USPTO, classes 9, 41, 42 and 44) and domains
(.com, .app, .bg). This is not a lawyer's clearance opinion.

## Preliminary ranking, before the registers

1. **Zakalka**: the only candidate whose everyday meaning is the product's idea,
   that a body is changed by the stress it meets, and the cold and load the app
   already logs are exactly what the word names. Distinctive in the US and EU,
   easy to say.
2. **Urutau**: literally "adapt", clean on the web, but carries the cultural
   caution above.
3. **Egoki**: short and clean, with a fuzzy-conflict risk around EGYM and "ego".
4. **Kaleno**: the same idea as Zakalka, softer to say, with the "muddy" reading
   in Bulgarian.

Stage 2 fills in the register and domain results and may reorder this list.
