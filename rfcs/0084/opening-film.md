# RFC 0084: three proposals for an opening film

Peter asked on 2026-10-05 for the page's first screen to open on a short film
in the style of the Opus-made "Prometheus" film, telling Tekiō's own story in
the app's look. These are the three storylines he was shown, each sketched as
a live rough cut. Which one, if any, is his call
([Unresolved questions](../0084-public-landing-site.md#unresolved-questions)).

## The reference

"Prometheus" is a two-minute film on Western civilization made by Claude Opus
5.5. Films like it are drawn in code rather than filmed or generated: each
scene is a script that paints the frame from the time alone, and a browser
records the frames. Its look is "a surveyor's plate mixed with a title card":
gold line-work on black or aged paper, Latin and Greek chapter marks, a
timeline ticking along the bottom, a counter that creeps upward, short
all-caps captions, and a last word, ACCELERATE. Sources:
[Blurred Culture](https://blurredculture.com/i-asked-claude-to-make-a-video-on-western-civiization/),
[explainx on the film](https://explainx.ai/blog/claude-opus-5-5-western-civilization-video-2026),
[explainx on how such films are made](https://explainx.ai/blog/how-opus-5-5-viral-videos-are-made-code-not-diffusion-2026).

What carries over is the grammar: Latin chapter marks that arrive as title
cards and pin to the corner, the ticking timeline, the climbing counter, and
line-work that redraws itself twelve times a second. What changes: Tekiō's
paper, ink and one accent instead of gold on black (a dark "ink" plate in the
app's colours is the nearer variant); captions in the page's sentence case; a
printer's misregistration on moving numbers instead of chromatic aberration;
and the last word is "Adapt."

## 1. Thin air (recommended)

The sweep, about 45 seconds in full. One line, the horizon, carries every
chapter.

| At | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Paper and a horizon. TEKIŌ · 適応, Japanese for adaptation. |
| 0:02 | I · ALTITUDO. A mountain in engraved cross-section; a route climbs it as the counter runs to 8,849 metres. "Thin air." |
| 0:07 | II · PROFUNDUM. The frame drops through sea level; a plumb line sinks ten metres, where the water presses twice as hard as the air above it. "Deep water." |
| 0:12 | III · VIA LONGA. The surface becomes the far end of a road; 42.195 kilometres go by. "The long road." |
| 0:17 | IV · ORBITA. The horizon bends into the edge of the Earth as the counter climbs 400 kilometres. "No gravity." |
| 0:22 | V · CORPUS. The app's body map draws itself. "You can adapt to almost anything." A squat, a row and a run land on it as ink. "What you do is what triggers it." |
| 0:28 | VI · LACUNA. The read: hamstrings never trained, calves short, VO₂max untouched. "Tekiō tells you what's missing." |
| 0:32 | "Adapt." |

Before it ships: each of the four numbers needs a source. None claims anything
about training, but the film implies that a body adapts to each place, so that
sentence goes through `/ground` like any other claim. It is the most drawing
work of the three.

## 2. The plate

The mechanism, about 30 seconds: an anatomical plate that fills itself in.

| At | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | A double rule, TABVLA I, and the app's figure drawn line by line. "You can adapt to almost anything." |
| 0:05 | I · STIMVLVS. A bench press, four sets of eight: each set inks the chest, and the front delts and triceps at half weight, as the app credits it. "What you do is what triggers it." |
| 0:10 | II · RECVPERATIO. The hatch lies over the muscles that worked and lifts as the days pass. "Ink is the work. Hatch is how recent." |
| 0:15 | III · DIES XIV. Two weeks of invented sessions fill the map and the whole-body squares. "Every set lands somewhere." |
| 0:22 | IV · LACVNA. Hamstrings never trained, calves short, VO₂max untouched. "Tekiō tells you what's missing." |
| 0:27 | "Adapt." |

The least drawing work. Its weakness: act one of the page tells the same story
a scroll later.

## 3. A year in ink

The hunt, about 40 seconds: one invented athlete's year.

| At | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | The body map, the seven qualities and a strip of 52 weeks draw themselves. "You can adapt to almost anything." |
| 0:03 | I · ROBUR, weeks 1 to 16. Heavy lifting: strength fills; power and the three whole-body qualities stay untouched. "Chase what you want most." "The rest stays untouched." |
| 0:14 | II · PATIENTIA, weeks 17 to 32. Long runs: endurance fills, and the upper body fades out of the read. "Chase something else, and the read shows what you left behind." |
| 0:25 | III · OMNIA, weeks 33 to 52. All seven fill; one muscle stays short. "Out of the box, Tekiō chases all seven." "And it tells you what's missing." |
| 0:36 | "Adapt." |

The real cut would run an invented year of sessions through the app's own
rules, as the page's example week does. The read fades when training stops
because it counts recent work only; the words must not turn that into a claim
about losing fitness.

## For all three

Drawn in code, one script painting each frame from the time alone, so the page
plays it live with no video to stream, and the same script renders video files
for social posts (frames captured by a headless browser, joined with ffmpeg).
It plays once, muted, and comes to rest on the first screen's words; reduced
motion shows only the resting frame; phones get an upright cut. Every athlete,
session and set in it is invented and says so on screen.

Why Thin air: the rest of the page already explains the read and plays a week
through the app's rules, so the first screen is the one place that says what
Tekiō believes.
